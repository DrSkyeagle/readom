# What is not finished

This list answers one question: **what is still missing.** Four groups -- changed and waiting for the device, verifications not yet run, designed but not built, and what has been verified on the device for contrast. Trade-offs that have been decided and will not change are in `known-issues.en.md`.

Everything is developed and measured on one Paperwhite 3 (stock firmware 5.16.2.1.1), so "verified on the device" always means that one.
